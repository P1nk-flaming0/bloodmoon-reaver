		      Section I. About
		      ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This mod adds new kit "Shapeshifter Werewolf" into the game. 
The purpose of this work is to restore and refine, the greatly underbalanced 
shapeshifter kit. The mod does NOT replaces the vanilla shapeshifter kit, 
so saved games using vanilla kit should be safe.




		    Section II. Installation
		    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
BGII Throne of Bhall is required. Shapeshifter REDUX Mod should work
fine with any WeiDU mod. 

Unzip the main ZIP file into your BGII main directory. This is normally:
        C:\Program Files\Black Isle\BGII - SoA\

Run (click on) "Setup-weredruid.exe". 

Then choose which components you would like to install. You may always
uninstall them later by re-running "Setup-weredruid.exe". 

You do *not* need to start a new game to take advantage of anything you
installed. 



		     Section III. Description
		     ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Kit Description:

SHAPESHIFTER WEREWOLF: This druid is not called Shapeshifter because he 
has access to a great variety of forms, rather because of his complete 
dedication to a single alternate form.  This druid has willingly allowed 
himself to become infected with Lycanthropy, but due to intense study and 
training he has the ability to control his affliction.  The creature he 
becomes is that of the Werewolf, the most famous of the lycanthrope shape 
changers.

Advantages:
-  May shapeshift into a wolf once per day for every 2 levels  
   (starts at 1st level with one use)

-  May shapeshift into the form of a werewolf once per day for every 2 levels  
   (starts at 4th level with one use)

-  At 14th level gains the ability to change into a greater werewolf

Disadvantages:
-  No other shapeshifting abilities due to the effort required maintaining 
   balance in his primary forms

-  Cannot wear ANY armor




High Level Abilities Description:


  *Howl
While in hybrid form, the werewolf realeses a gruesome howl, striking fear 
in the hearts of nearby enemies. All opponents in a 30' radius must save vs 
spell with -6 penalty or flee in panic.


 *Feral Rage
Allowing himself to be consumed by his feral insticts, the werewolf enters a 
trance of animalistic rage.

-  While enraged: Strenght rises by 2, claws hit as a +5 weapon, movement and attack 
   are increased.
-  While enraged: Immune to charm, hold and fear, maze, imprisonment, stun and sleep.

Requires: Howl


  *Additional Rage #1
Adds an extra rage attack

Requires: Feral Rage


  *Additional Rage #2
Adds an extra rage attack

Requires: Additional Rage #1



High Level Abilities Removed:

  *Earth Elemental Transformation
  *Fire Elemental Transformation


Special Abilities:

  *Respawn Claws
Does what it says. It's sole purpose is to deal with the "Dispel/Remove Magic"
problem.


Shapeshifting Stats:

Wolf form:
Str 18
Dex 17
Con 17
Claws hit as magical weapon
CLaws Damage 1d6+2
APR 2 (Attacks per Round)
Base AC 6


Werewolf form
Str 19
Dex 16
Con 15
Claws hit as +2 weapon
CLaws Damage 2d4+4
APR 2
Base AC 1
Magic Resistance 20 


Greater Werewolf form
Str 21
Dex 20
Con 25
Claws hit as +4 weapon
Calws Damage 2d8+6
APR 2
Base AC -6
Magic Resistance 40
Elemental Resistance 50
Saves 1/1/1/2/1 
Immunity to normal weapons 
Regeneration of 1 HP per 10 seconds

THAC0 is progressional in all forms




		       Section IV. Known Issues
		       ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Every transformation to werewolf resets the abilities of the hybrid form to 
their maximum value. Not a bug, but more of a limitation of my programming skills, 
feel free to contact me if you are willing to suggest a way around this.
